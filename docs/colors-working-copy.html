Colors

Shared color reference for the whole app. Principal hues anchor surfaces, buttons, and text. This standalone page is an editing workspace; it does not depend on Storybook.

1. Principal colors

Primary keeps a 40° hue and Secondary a 218° hue. Principal colors use 100% saturation. Light theme targets 56.5% relative luminance. Dark Primary and Secondary have each been darkened by another 10%; their current relative luminance is 22.0% and 22.6%, respectively.

Light Primary · 40°#ffb92e · 56.2%
Light Secondary · 218°#a7c8ff · 56.7%
Dark Primary · 40°#b07500 · 22.0%
Dark Secondary · 218°#4b80e6 · 22.6%

Use principal colors as hue anchors. Adjust saturation and luminance for each role while retaining the Primary or Secondary hue.

2. Surface colors

Surface colors are for non-button backgrounds, including text fields and text areas. Each theme has Neutral and Active sets with six levels. Level 0 is deepest; level 5 is lightest. Active levels use the Primary hue at 100% saturation and match the corresponding neutral level's luminance.

The light neutral set below reflects the requested recalibration: level 0 is #d1d1d1, with equal RGB increments through level 5 at #eeeeee.

  • Page background: level 1.
  • A surface on a page: level 2. A surface within that container: level 3. Continue one level at a time through level 4. Cards that group swatch sets use level 2; variant cards nested inside them use level 3.
  • Sticky or fixed surfaces: level 5.
  • Choose Neutral for ordinary surfaces and Active where the same depth needs the Primary hue. Buttons have their own palette.

3. Button colors

Primary and Secondary use their principal hue at 100% saturation. Neutral uses the surface color at the level it occupies, instead of always using level 1. Gradients go from +5% luminance at the top, through that surface color, to −5% at the bottom. The top stop spans 0–10%, the middle stop is at 90%, and the bottom stop is at 100%. Hover brightens by 5%; press darkens by 5% and reverses the gradient.

Focus uses the foreground for Primary and Secondary, and Primary for Neutral. Disabled buttons use solid backgrounds at 5% saturation, have no border, and have no hover, press, or keyboard focus.

4. Text colors

Default and Bold are distinct roles. To maintain the intended cross-theme contrast, light-theme headings use Dark Primary and inline links use Dark Secondary; dark-theme headings use Light Primary and inline links use Light Secondary. Muted text is selected per surface for at least 4.5:1 contrast with normal text.

Interaction color meanings

  • Background: default fill for a surface or control.
  • Foreground: text, icon, or other content on that surface.
  • Border: boundary or outline around an element.
  • Hover: pointer-over feedback.
  • Press: active interaction feedback, where supported.
  • Focus: visible keyboard-focus indicator; shape and placement belong to each component.
  • Disabled: unavailable interaction; disabled buttons use a solid 5%-saturation fill with no border and do not respond to hover or press.

Colors define shared visual meaning. Components define dimensions, spacing, shadows, and interaction behavior.
